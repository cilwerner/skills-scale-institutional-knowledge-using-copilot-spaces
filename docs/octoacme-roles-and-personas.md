# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsors

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide executive direction and champion the business need for an initiative. A sponsor may be a stakeholder or executive who is accountable for ensuring the project remains aligned with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business case, desired outcomes, and strategic alignment
- Secure or escalate resource and funding needs
- Make or escalate major scope, priority, and funding decisions
- Remove organizational blockers and support timely decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Project Manager on escalations, resource constraints, and major delivery decisions
- Align with the Product Manager on desired outcomes, priority, and changes to the business case
- Provide Developers and QA/Testing with timely stakeholder decisions when trade-offs affect delivery

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction and help ensure that solutions are feasible, secure, maintainable, and compatible with relevant systems.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, technical design, and integration decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and non-functional requirements
- Support estimation and break down complex technical work
- Promote engineering standards and review significant technical changes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers on design, implementation, reviews, and technical problem-solving
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on estimates, dependencies, and technical risks
- Partner with the Product Manager to explain technical trade-offs that affect scope, timing, or user outcomes
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on testability and coverage of technical requirements

---

## UX / Research Leads

### Role Summary
UX and Research Leads help teams understand user needs and translate them into accessible, usable product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct appropriate user research and usability validation
- Develop experience flows, interaction designs, and accessibility guidance
- Identify usability risks and communicate evidence-based recommendations
- Contribute user-centered acceptance criteria and design artifacts

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to validate problems, user needs, and product priorities
- Coordinate with the Project Manager to plan research and design activities within delivery milestones
- Work with Developers to implement feasible, accessible designs
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to validate usability and accessibility against acceptance criteria

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads ensure that teams can measure whether a project delivers its intended outcomes and that the resulting data is reliable.

### Responsibilities
- Define instrumentation and measurement plans aligned to success metrics
- Establish event definitions, data quality checks, and reporting approaches
- Analyze results and communicate insights and limitations
- Identify privacy or governance considerations in measurement

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to define and evaluate outcome metrics
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on measurement dependencies and reporting milestones
- Work with Developers to implement instrumentation and with QA/Testing to verify it
- Share findings with Stakeholders to inform decisions and follow-up improvements

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise teams on protecting systems and data and meeting applicable security and privacy requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy risks, requirements, and review checkpoints
- Advise on appropriate controls and data-handling practices
- Coordinate or conduct risk reviews and communicate findings
- Recommend mitigations and escalate significant unresolved risks

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers on secure design, implementation, and remediation
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to include security and privacy requirements in validation
- Coordinate with the Project Manager to track reviews, risks, mitigations, and escalations
- Advise the Product Manager and Stakeholders on risks that affect scope, user trust, or release decisions

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release and Operations Leads coordinate production readiness and help ensure that delivered changes can be deployed, monitored, supported, and recovered safely.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release readiness, deployment planning, and operational handoffs
- Define monitoring and post-deployment verification needs
- Prepare support and rollback or mitigation plans
- Identify operational risks and readiness gaps before release

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, and readiness status
- Work with Developers on deployment, monitoring, and recovery procedures
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on smoke tests and post-deployment verification
- Partner with the Product Manager and Stakeholders on release communications and operational impact

---

## Assigning Roles
- Assign roles explicitly during initiation or planning so responsibilities and decision ownership are clear.
- A person may hold more than one role on a smaller project; not every project needs a separate person for every role.
- Involve specialist roles when the project's risks, users, data, technical complexity, or release needs call for them.
