# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme's project management guidance for cross-functional projects, from validating an initiative through delivery, release, and continuous improvement.

## Project management processes

OctoAcme follows an iterative lifecycle. Teams initiate work by confirming the business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, timeline, risks, and available resources. Once aligned, they plan by prioritizing a backlog, defining acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, estimating work, and mapping milestones, dependencies, and risks.

During execution, teams deliver in small increments and track progress on a project board. Project Managers coordinate schedules, dependencies, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Standups, delivery syncs, regular PM/Product Manager alignment, and stakeholder updates keep progress and blockers visible, while risks are reviewed and escalated as needed.

Quality checks continue through release: teams use unit and integration tests where appropriate, automated CI tests and linting, and security scanning. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security checks, release notes, a rollback or mitigation plan, and smoke tests. Teams verify deployments, communicate releases, and respond to incidents with triage and rollback when needed. After each sprint, release, or milestone, retrospectives turn lessons into a few prioritized, owned improvement actions.
